# YTIS Safety Boundaries

YTIS is a local-first AI source intelligence workbench for public-safe research, learning and portfolio evidence.

## Allowed use

YTIS is intended for:

- public-safe transcripts and source material;
- manually provided notes and documents that are safe to analyze;
- learning roadmaps and technical/business research;
- structured extraction, summarization and classification;
- human-reviewed action plans and reports;
- local saved analyses and knowledge cards.

## Not allowed or not intended

YTIS should not be used to:

- access private content without permission;
- work around paywalls, CAPTCHA, login walls or access controls;
- process real private customer data;
- store credentials or secrets;
- publish copyrighted source text verbatim;
- present AI output as certain fact;
- make decisions without human review;
- claim production MLOps maturity;
- claim senior AI engineering capability;
- act as an automated business decision-maker.

## Human review rule

Every generated answer must be reviewed by a human before it becomes a saved analysis, knowledge card, report or action plan.

## Evidence rule

YTIS should separate source evidence, AI interpretation and human decisions. When evidence is weak, the output should say so.

## Public portfolio rule

Only public-safe sample data, screenshots and reports should be published. Runtime data, generated source packs, local project folders and private analyses must stay out of Git unless deliberately sanitized.
